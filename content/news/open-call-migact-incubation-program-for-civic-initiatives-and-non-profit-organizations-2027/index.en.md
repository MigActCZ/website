Are you active in Prague’s civic life and ready to launch or grow your initiative? Join MigAct’s Incubation Program — a free, 30+ hour capacity-building journey designed to help informal groups and civic organizations strengthen their skills, connect with peers, and amplify their impact.

**About the Program**

MigAct’s Incubation Program is aimed at supporting, professionalizing, and strengthening the capacities of informal groups and associations founded or led by people with a migration background— especially those including young adults (ages 18–30) living in Prague.

The goal of the program is to support projects and activities that contribute to greater belonging in civil society, develop and connect local communities, encourage active citizenship, or pursue other public and socially beneficial goals (such as environmental protection, supporting vulnerable population groups, and more).
