**Location:** Prague (Office-based with flexible work from home upon agreement)
**Duration:** November, 2026- September 2027 (with potential for long-term collaboration)
**Contract:** Part-time (DPP / Contract), -20 hrs/month (higher workload Nov 2026 - Feb 2027)
**Pay:** CZK 400 / hour

**About the Role:**

Help us train the next generation of community organizers in Prague! We’re recruiting a Community Organizing Specialist to design, align, and evaluate a training curriculum tailored to local community organizing needs as part of an international Erasmus+ project (3 partner countries). This is a unique opportunity to shape the training of future community organizers, co-create learning methodologies, and contribute to the growth of local civil society within an international framework.

**Key Responsibilities:**

**Curriculum Design & Enhancement:** Co-create and refine the curriculum for a multi-country community organizing training program.

**Trainer Support:** Communicate regularly with trainers to align learning outcomes, educational methods, and content delivery.

**Methodological Support:** Deliver ongoing methodological guidance for educational activities and provide direct mentoring to participants.

**Participant Recruitment:** Support the recruitment strategy and selection process for training participants.

**Evaluation & Data Analysis:** Co-create evaluation tools for the training program, analyze data, and synthesize findings.

**What We Are Looking For:**

- Proven experience working on community organizing topics (preferably in Prague or across Czechia).
- Demonstrated experience in designing educational curricula, learning outcomes, and training methods.
- Practical experience with program evaluation and data analysis.
- Solid understanding of the local Czech civil society context.
- Ability to work across diverse cultural backgrounds and within international partnerships.
- Advanced proficiency in English (spoken and written) - the training program will be conducted in English to engage community organizers from migrant backgrounds.
- Free access to the Czech labor market (valid work permit/visa or citizenship permitting work under DPP/contract in Czechia)

**What We Offer:**

**Meaningful Work:** Help build stronger, more resilient communities across Europe through targeted educational programs.

**Co-Creation & Autonomy:** An innovative, friendly and diverse NGO culture where your ideas matter.

**Flexibility:** Part-time engagement (\~25 hours/month on average, with increased intensity from Nov 2026 to Feb 2027), flexible working hours.

**Future Growth:** Opportunity for long-term cooperation.

**How to Apply:**

In case you are interested, please send your **CV and a short motivation letter in English** to Natallia Allen at natallia@migact.net by **October 23rd, 2026.** Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis. Ideal start date is November 1st, 2026.
