Join us for a “Community Networking Brunch” to meet, connect and learn from your neighbours, active groups, community workers, community workers, non-profits, and local initiatives in Prague 7!

Also, there will be space for you to talk about your initiatives, ideas, and interests.

Let’s meet each other and get more involved in the neighbourhood!

Light brunch, coffee, tea and refreshments will be provided— no need to bring anything but yourself!

Don’t forget to register — we’re excited to meet you!

The event is free to attend. This project is supported by MigAct, Agora 7, Prague 7 Municipality and the Ministry of Interior of the Czech Republic.
