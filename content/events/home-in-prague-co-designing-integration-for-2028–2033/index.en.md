**Have Your Say on the Prague Integration Strategy 2028–2033!**

The City of Prague, in cooperation with the Prague Integration Centre, is preparing a new **Prague Integration Strategy for 2028–2033**. This document will help define what Prague should focus on in the coming years to truly be a city for everyone – a place where all residents can live well and feel that they belong.

When building this strategy, we want to look beyond data and expert opinions—we want to shape it together with you. Your experiences and ideas are essential to shaping the future of life in Prague. So we'd love to know: What makes everyday life in Prague easier for you? What challenges do you face? Most importantly, what concrete solutions would you propose, and what needs to change?

The Strategy focuses on five main areas: [public institutions](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XaIyYazEXQoZyD1Yfk79n3uPCgnOZk0Ik8i2xXUK4mM/edit?usp=sharing), [access to services and support](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1VB2OWvzEL2oTM2PBCCtflgFqKBMnZ1_q5oHZ-NvHhFQ/edit?usp=sharing), [information and navigating life in Prague](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1h1ErJVCxOQjnW_THTIzh_AxMYp6q-34V86ocUzUF-gg/edit?usp=sharing), [civic engagement and living together in the city,](https://docs.google.com/document/d/14TKyYiy_TwplGufUCNKOpJ7ujjJrA9lbMyJxI46HgBQ/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.u0gpylqx6hqq) and [education](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19RVwDJPH6GkB7yyXKM2OjeBioPYofZOeeKnIxmwR_5A/edit?usp=drive_link) (links lead to [preliminary draft texts](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/16-dRxHMNAd-Uy2HDD1Ni7QKsUTqPNZkK)).

Here’s how you can join:

1. Take the Questionnaire (15 mins) [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfhfywFV60zcz4TbRoi3C6garm018emFPAOzOOcbRVLVNc3gA/viewform).
Share your feedback and concrete ideas to help us understand what Prague should prioritize in the coming years. The survey is available in English and Czech and is open through September 27, 2026.
2. Join our in-person participatory meeting. 
Discuss your ideas directly with us! **The session will be held in English.**

Thank you for helping us make Prague a city where everyone can live well and feel that they belong. 💙
