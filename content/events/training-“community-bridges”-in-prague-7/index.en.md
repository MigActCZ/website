We would like to invite you to a five-workshop training programme, which will train residents and those interested in Prague 7 to become  “Community bridges” for the neighbourhood. The workshops will dive deep into 5-key-focus areas of strengthening community connections:

1. Learn the know-how of Prague 7 neighbourhood–October 19 (Monday) from 18-21 hrs at Přistav 7 
2. Communication skills–October 20 (Tuesday) from 18-21 hrs at Přistav 7
3. Stress management and burnout prevention–October 23 (Friday) from 18-21 hrs at Studánka-Komunitní prostor Letná.
4. Fundraising for local projects–October 26 (Monday) from 18-21 hrs at Přistav 7 
5. Roadmap and workflow creation–October 27 (Tuesday) from 18-21 hrs at Přistav 7

With expert facilitators, participants will explore, learn, and practice skills to act as “Community bridges” to support newcomers in the neighbourhood, including both Czechs and residents with a migration background, to strengthen the community. All workshops are free. 

**Language:** ENG (Level of English will be basic)

This project is supported by MigAct, Agora 7, Prague 7 Municipality and the Ministry of Interior of the Czech Republic.

Coffee, tea and light refreshments will be provided!

Don’t forget to register—we’re excited to meet you!
