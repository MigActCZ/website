**The Integration Centre Prague invites you to the 71st Regional Advisory Platform on Cooperation Between Municipal Districts and Non-Profit Organizations, which will take place on Thursday, November 19, 2026, from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM in the Architects' Hall (Sál architektů),** [**Old Town Square 1, Prague 1**](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Starom%C4%9Bstsk%C3%A9+n%C3%A1m.+1)**.**

The November platform will be dedicated to presenting projects that focus directly on mutual cooperation between municipal districts and non-profit organizations or can help municipal districts address current challenges. 

Through its IntegraPolis projects, [the Consortium of Migrant-Assisting NGOs](https://www.konsorcium-nno.cz/) helps cities and municipal districts map the needs of foreigners and local actors, identify integration visions, and subsequently implement them into local policies. 

The organization [MigAct](https://migact.cz/), in cooperation with municipal districts, creates a favorable environment for civic participation (not only) for residents with a migration background. [The Association for Integration and Migration (SIMI)](https://migrace.com/) and the organization [La Strada](https://www.strada.cz/) respond through their projects to the growing number of young people from Ukraine at risk of labor exploitation and human trafficking.

### **Program**

**1:00 PM – 1:30 PM | Updates from Prague City Hall and ICP**

Updates from the Department of National Minorities and Foreigners of the Prague City Hall and the Integration Centre Prague.

- IntegraPolis: The Future of Integration in Cities and the Follow-up Project — Ondřej Valenta, Consortium of Migrants Assisting NNOs in the Czech Republic.
- Supporting Community and Civic Participation in Prague 3 and Prague 7 — Mónica Márquez Bobadilla and Natallia Allen, MigAct.

**2:40 PM – 3:10 PM | Break**

Space for networking and refreshments.

3:10 PM – 4:00 PM | Presentations with Discussion

- Projects Supporting Ukrainian Youth — Aišvarja Eassey, Association for Integration and Migration.
- Presentation of Other Current NGO Projects

_Program subject to change._
