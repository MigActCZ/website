MigAct is starting its own series of DEMOS Dialogues! 🗣️✨

In a polarized world filled with information overload and screen-based interactions, we invite you to meet in person, slow down, and engage in real, meaningful conversation.

This first session of our new meeting series is an open space where we’ll dive deep into key societal issues using structured DEMOS methodology. 

The goal isn’t to win debates or force a consensus, but to build mutual understanding and connection—even around complex topics.

Together, we’ll choose from 5 key focus areas:
📊 Economic Inequality
🎓 Education
⚖️ Rule of Law
🤝 Trust
🚀 Future of Work

💬 Languages: English & Czech. Czech speakers have the option to join a dialogue session exclusively in Czech!

🗓️ Event Schedule:
\* 18:00 – Doors open
\* 18:15 – Intro & topic selection
\* 18:30 – Guided dialogues session
\* 19:40 – Closing thoughts
\* 20:00 – Networking & casual chat
