Let’s meet for the second edition of MigActs own DEMOS Dialogues! Let’s talk. And listen.

We will start by choosing together one of the following topics:
• Voting
• Youth Participation
• Rule of Law
• Accountability
• Learning from History

And if time allows, we can touch a few topics!

Do you know DEMOS methodology already?

It provides support and facilitation to the dialogues so that they can flow and dive deeper into the topic, even when it might be uncomfortable. The goal is to improve our understanding of each other rather than reaching a consensus or winning a debate.

Come try it!
