The project aims to strengthen civic participation in diverse urban communities by building the competences of local community organisers, with a focus on residents with migration backgrounds. The project will develop skills in intercultural communication, inclusive organising, leadership and fundraising, while building a sustainable transnational peer-learning network and strengthening the methodological capacities of partner organisations. 

**Activities:**

Partners will co-design an educational curriculum for community organizers and pilot the programme in Prague, Berlin and Budapest. The training will combine non-formal education, experiential learning and peer exchange, with particular outreach to residents with migration backgrounds. Participants will also engage in online transnational peer-learning sessions. The project will conclude with the publication of a digital toolkit.
